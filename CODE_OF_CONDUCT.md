# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in our project and community a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include being respectful, accepting constructive feedback, helping other community members, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, and publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards of this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and in public spaces when an individual is representing the project or its community.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers. All reports will be reviewed and handled fairly.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers will consider the nature and impact of each incident and take appropriate corrective action.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is inspired by established codes of conduct used by open-source communities.
